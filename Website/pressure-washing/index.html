How we do it

Different surfaces need different pressure. Concrete and interlock can take a strong clean, while wood decks and fences need a lighter touch so the grain isn’t damaged. We set up for each surface before we start.

For storefronts and commercial walkways, we can work early or after hours so your customers aren’t in the way.

Pressure Washing questions

Will pressure washing damage my deck?

Not when it’s done right. We use lower pressure on wood and adjust for each surface.

When is the best time for pressure washing?

Spring through early fall, while temperatures stay above freezing.

Do you need water from my house?

We’ll need access to an outdoor tap. Let us know when you book if that’s an issue.
