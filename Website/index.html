Why our windows dry spotless

Tap water leaves minerals on glass as it dries. We take them out first.

  1. Filter

    Tap water runs through a 3-stage deionizing filter on our rig.

  2. Scrub

    Purified water feeds a soft brush on a long pole that cleans the glass and frames from the ground.

  3. Rinse and dry

    With nothing dissolved in the water, it dries clear. No soap, no squeegee, no film.
